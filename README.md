# MKT202: Marketing Management Project
## TAAGA - "Wear Now, Pay Later" Service Launch Strategy

This repository contains the comprehensive marketing presentation developed for the course **MKT202 (Marketing Management)** at **North South University** back in 2024.

### 📌 Project Overview
The project introduces a strategic implementation of the **"Wear Now, Pay Later" (BNPL)** credit system for **TAAGA**, a trendy sub-brand of **Aarong Bangladesh (BRAC-Aarong)**. The service focuses on offering financial flexibility and payment convenience primarily targeted toward university students and young urban professionals.

### 👨‍🏫 Faculty
* **Faculty:** FDF
* **Institution:** North South University
* **Timeline:** 2023

### 📊 Key Marketing Dimensions Covered
* **STP Analysis:** Targeting young urban women (aged 18-35), college students, and cultural appreciators.
* **Competitor Mapping:** Direct contrast with brands like Yellow, Ecstasy, Kay Kraft, and Deshal.
* **Marketing Mix (4Ps):** 
  * **Product:** Financial integration within physical outlets.
  * **Price:** Market penetration strategy (Free for the first 3 months).
  * **Place:** Exclusive zero-level distribution channels via physical TAAGA outlets.
  * **Promotion:** Driven by Word-of-Mouth, Social Media campaigns, and Direct Marketing.
